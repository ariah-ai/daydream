After the first hard frost the shed closes for the season. Every tool comes off the rack, gets wiped and oiled, and goes back. The rototiller is drained — the tank and the carburettor — and the tarp goes over it. The [[loan-log]] gets a line across the page with the date, and the key goes back to the coordinator until March.
